**Genspark에 먼저 붙여 넣기 ↓**
```
해설 페이지의 '프롬프트 연습 과제' 예상 결과 그림을 한 장씩 차례로 만들어 줘.

- 첨부한 참고 이미지의 캐릭터 외형(얼굴·머리·색·장식)을 똑같이 지킬 것

- '같은 대화에 이어서'라고 적힌 그림은 바로 앞 그림을 바탕으로 적힌 것만 바꿀 것
```

Art style: high-quality anime illustration, clean confident lineart, two-tone cel shading, natural adult proportions.
Match the attached reference images exactly (face, hair, colors, accessories). All characters are adults in their twenties (the Professor is an adult man in his thirties); modest outfits exactly as described.

Signal 1 — face outline: long face with a defined angular jaw and a square chin tip.
Signal 2 — eyes: narrow almond eyes about half as tall as they are wide, spaced one eye width apart.
Signal 3 — features: nose bottom slightly above one quarter of the head height, mouth one third of the way from the nose to the chin, ears from the upper eyelid to the nose tip.
Signal 4 — neck and shoulders: neck as wide as the distance between the pupils, shoulders about twice the head width. Light from the upper left.
Plain off-white studio background.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no revealing clothing, not childlike, no school uniform, not resembling any famous anime character.
